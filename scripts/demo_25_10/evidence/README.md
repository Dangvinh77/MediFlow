attempts, temporary process manifests and generated service output remain on the
demo machine and are ignored by Git. They are not deleted by this ignore rule.

`original.json` and `redelivered.json` files preserve their exact source bytes.

The JSON identifiers refer to synthetic acceptance or demo records. No access or
refresh tokens are included. Reports distinguish a fresh paid normal Lab flow,
snapshot/topology smoke, client checks and browser smoke; none implies every
