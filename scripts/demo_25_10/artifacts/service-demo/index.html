MediFlow02 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS2 minutes
MVP scope · multi-specialty outpatient care

MediFlow functional requirements

FR-01

Organization

Departments, staff and clinic rooms; email login, temporary passwords by email, account locking and department head appointments.

FR-02

Patient

Search by national ID before creating a record; avoid duplicates, update profiles, issue patient accounts and reset passwords.

FR-03

Intake · Clinical

Doctor shifts and session capacity; desk or advance registration, 15-minute reservations, follow-ups, room queue numbers and vital signs.

FR-04

Consultation · Clinical

Visit-specific medical records, ICD diagnoses and lab orders; complete records after labs finish or are cancelled; recommend follow-ups.

FR-05

Lab

Payment clearance before testing; specimens and result templates; separate entry and release staff; reference ranges, critical alerts and corrections.

FR-06

Pharmacy

Drug catalog, stock and low-stock alerts; prescriptions linked to open records, stock reservations and dispensing after payment.

FR-07

Billing

Quotes and simulated financial support; consultation, lab and medication payments; source-specific clearance, duplicate prevention, refunds and history.

FR-08

Notification · Report

Event-driven in-app notices; patients view their own results; outpatient reports for reconciled periods with department access limits.

CROSS-CUTTINGJWT + role-based access, deny by default · Idempotency-Key for writes · Outbox + RabbitMQ across contexts · one database per context
